Capture a whole site

Every page, with its images, videos and files

Verbatim visits each page of this site (from its sitemap and by following its links), saves it as Markdown in a folder that mirrors the site, and downloads the images, video files and documents into a shared media folder. YouTube and Vimeo players are linked, not downloaded.

It stays on this site, follows the site's robots.txt, and reads a few pages at a time. Keep this tab open until it finishes.
