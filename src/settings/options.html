Verbatim settings

Save captures to

Choose a folder, such as a folder in your Obsidian vault, and every capture is saved straight into it. Without one, captures go to Downloads/Verbatim.

Checking…

Chrome can ask you to re-allow access to the folder, for example after it restarts. Verbatim opens this page when that happens. Click Allow access again and choose Allow on every visit so Chrome remembers. Meanwhile, captures are saved to Downloads/Verbatim, so nothing is lost.
